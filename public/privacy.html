Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Cephalometry Studio has no accounts of its own and no server that stores your data. Your cases stay in your browser. They leave your device only if you export them or connect your own Google Drive, and we can't read them either way.

Who runs this site

Cephalometry Studio is a free, open-source tool for research and education, developed by Dr. Muhammad Nabeel Shaesha. For questions about this policy, email cephalometrystudio@gmail.com.

Data stored in your browser

Everything you enter is stored on your device: patient details you choose to add (such as name, ID, date of birth, age and sex), X-ray images, landmarks, measurements, studies and your settings.

  • Cases and images are encrypted in the browser's storage, where the browser supports it. The key stays on your device.
  • This data isn't sent to us. We have no copy and can't recover it if it is lost, so keep backups.
  • Clearing your browser's site data, or using "Clear all local data" in the app, deletes it from that browser.

AI Trace

AI Trace runs entirely in your browser. Your images aren't uploaded. The first time you use it, your browser downloads the AI model from Hugging Face and keeps a copy for later use.

Google Drive backup and sync

These features are optional and work only if you connect a Google account. There are no background uploads until you set them up: backups run when you click, and sync runs while the app is open on a profile you have linked.

When you connect Google Drive, the app can:

  • create files and folders in your Drive, and read, change and delete only the ones this app created (the drive.file permission). It can't see your other Drive files.
  • read your Google account's email address, to show which account is connected and check that a profile signs in to the right one. It is saved in your browser with that profile.

Your files travel directly between your browser and Google. They don't pass through us and we never receive them, your email address or your sign-in token. The token is kept in your browser only for the session and lasts about an hour.

Encryption is your choice. Backups and sync can each be encrypted in your browser with a passphrase before upload. We never receive the passphrase, so a lost passphrase can't be recovered. If you turn encryption off, patient details and images are stored in your Drive in readable form, and Google's terms and your Drive's sharing settings apply to them.

We don't sell Google user data, share it with anyone, or use it for advertising or to train AI models. Cephalometry Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

  • To stop access: use Disconnect in the app, or remove the app under Third-party connections in your Google Account.
  • To delete Drive data: delete the "Cephalometry Studio backups" and "Cephalometry Studio sync" folders from your Drive. Removing access doesn't delete them.
  • To delete local data: use "Clear all local data" in the app, or clear this site's data in your browser.

Other services your browser contacts

These services receive normal connection details from your browser, such as your IP address. They don't receive your cases or images, except Google Drive as described above.

  • Vercel hosts this site and may keep standard server logs.
  • Hugging Face serves the AI model download.
  • GitHub serves community norms and examples. If you contribute a preset, you submit it through your own GitHub account, publicly.
  • Google provides sign-in and Drive, and only if you use those features.

The site doesn't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Patient data

If you enter information about other people, you are responsible for having the right to use it under the laws and rules that apply to you, including consent and ethics approval. The app includes anonymization tools for exports. Cephalometry Studio is for research and education and isn't cleared for clinical diagnosis or treatment planning.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. The project is open source, so you can check how the app handles data in its source code.
